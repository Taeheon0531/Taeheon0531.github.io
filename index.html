About

I'm a senior majoring in Molecular Biology at Ghent University Global Campus. I'm especially interested in immunology and in how healthcare systems work, from the lab bench to the patient.

Python is my strongest programming language, and I'm comfortable building machine learning models with it. I've also contributed to health informatics research on electronic health records and health IT usability.
